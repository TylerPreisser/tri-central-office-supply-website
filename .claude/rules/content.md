# Content & brand conventions

- `SITE_PLAN.md` is the binding brand/design brief for this revision — read it before any copy
  or visual change. Section 1 ("Brand Read") lists directions the client explicitly rejected:
  bottom-pill mobile nav as primary, a mobile utility bar, hero-card templates, rounded-box as
  the dominant visual language, a random product image as brand foundation, and stacked-desktop
  leftovers on mobile. Don't silently reintroduce any of these.
- Brand color roles are fixed (`SITE_PLAN.md` "Actual brand system"): navy = trust anchor, red =
  decisive service/order action, blue = digital catalog tools, warm neutral = furniture/
  workplace guidance only. Don't repurpose a role color for an unrelated section.
- Section backgrounds are section-owned per the plan's alignment rule: white starts with
  header/hero, panel-gray starts with category/quick-task utilities, warm neutral is scoped to
  the furniture/machine section only, navy starts with footer/contact. Don't let a background
  bleed behind an unrelated module.
- The category index and service ledger are deliberately rule-based rows, not
  cards/pills/boxes (`SITE_PLAN.md` §3, "Non-card component"). Don't convert them to a card
  grid as a "modernization."
- Phone number, hours, and address in the utility bar (`index.html:11-16`) are real business
  contact info — verify against the source before ever changing them, not just format them.
